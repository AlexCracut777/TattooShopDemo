Aftercare guide

Heal it right

A tattoo is only as good as its healing. Here's the first month, step by step.

Day one

Leave it, then wash it

  • Keep the wrap on for as long as your artist tells you.
  • Wash gently with lukewarm water and unscented soap. Clean hands only.
  • Pat dry with clean paper towel, then let it air for a few minutes.
  • Put on a very thin layer of the aftercare balm we recommend.
Week one

Keep it clean, keep it dry

  • Wash two or three times a day and keep the balm layer thin.
  • No baths, swimming pools, sea or saunas. Quick showers are fine.
  • Keep it out of the sun, and wear loose clothes over it.
  • It will flake and peel. Don't pick it or scratch it.
Weeks two to four

Let it settle

  • Itching and a cloudy look are normal while the skin settles.
  • Keep moisturising once a day with an unscented lotion.
  • Once it's fully healed, use SPF 50 on it every time it's in the sun.
  • Come back within 8 weeks for your free touch-up check.
If something feels wrong, get it checked. Redness that keeps spreading, heat, pus or a fever aren't part of normal healing. See a doctor or pharmacist, then let us know.
